Precision Processing

Our ISO 22000 certified facility ensures maximum safety and unparalleled freshness at every production step.

Tuna Loin Preparation Cycle

Executed strictly according to our certified HACCP manual.

1

Receiving raw material and rigorous quality checking.

2

Deheading the tuna to prepare for filleting.

3

Precision tuna cutting by expert handlers.

4

Careful skinning of the tuna flesh.

5

Removing all bones and dark meat during trimming.

6

Wrapping fillets safely using food grade wrapping papers.

7

Measuring each individual piece and applying labels.

8

Master carton packing using internal polythene and gel ice.

9

Final products stored in our cool room to maintain the exact required temperature.

Headed and Gutted Workflow

Graded exclusively to United States quality standards.

1

Receiving raw material with immediate temperature checks.

2

Grading the tuna exactly as per United States standards.

3

Deheading the tuna to prepare for internal cleaning.

4

Washing the tuna internally using pure chill water.

5

Measuring the precise weight before the packing stage.

6

Packing the headed and gutted tuna carefully into cartons.

7

Placing gel ice directly inside the belly cavity for rapid cooling.

8

Preparing the product for the final master carton.

9

Sealing inside master cartons with inner polythene and extra gel ice.

10

Extracting tail cut samples for final quality verification.
